# 知识幕布

## 中文

承担验密与验规的发布前最后质检机构，分通用、专业、企业三级。发布前最后一道防线，既脱敏又合规，守住隐私与法律底线。

## English

The final pre-release quality gate handling desensitization and rule compliance, at three levels: general, professional, and enterprise. The last line of defense before publishing — desensitized and compliant, protecting privacy and legal boundaries.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 对外文章、方案发布前过幕布
- 宣传物料发布前过幕布
