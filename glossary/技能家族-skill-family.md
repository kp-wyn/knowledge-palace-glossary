# 技能家族

## 中文

围绕同一使用范式、命名风格统一、可互相链接成体系的一组技能。单个技能独立引流，家族化命名与互链能形成认知合力、带动整套体系传播。

## English

A group of skills sharing the same usage paradigm, consistent naming style, and cross-linkable into a system. Individual skills attract users independently; family naming and cross-links create cognitive momentum that propagates the whole system.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 「X一下」家族：记一下、收拾一下、消化一下、理一下 / "X-me" family: note-it, tidy-it, digest-it, sort-it
