# 知识治理

## 中文

对知识全生命周期做体系化管理：质量分级、权限隔离、更新淘汰、合规审查与权属界定。宫殿规模扩大后，单靠日常整理无法保证质量、安全与合规。

## English

Systematic management of the knowledge lifecycle: quality grading, permission isolation, pruning, compliance review, and ownership definition. As the palace scales, daily tidying alone can't guarantee quality, security, and compliance.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 质量分级、权限隔离
- 过期淘汰、合规审查
