# 企业级知识宫殿

## 中文

服务组织经营决策、对外输出与知识资产沉淀的知识宫殿，强调组织协同、权限隔离与整体合规。企业的知识分散在个人手里无法复用，企业宫殿把知识变成组织资产。

## English

A Knowledge Palace serving organizational decisions, external output, and knowledge asset accumulation — emphasizing collaboration, permission isolation, and overall compliance. Enterprise knowledge scattered in individuals' heads can't be reused; the enterprise palace turns it into organizational assets.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 组织知识库、对外输出合规
- 多岗位、多殿协同
