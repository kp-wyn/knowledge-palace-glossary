# 知识白盒

## 中文

AI 生成内容的内部推理过程、调用的知识来源、使用的 SOP 步骤全部可见可查 —— 产出物稳定可控、可解释、可追溯，但尚未注入创作者个人的文风、经验判断与故事感。交付物 "正确但平庸"，像一个合格的实习生写的：专业但没有你的指纹。

### 为什么叫 "白盒"

借自软件工程的 "白盒测试"—— 盒子透明了，里面的逻辑你看得见。从盲盒到白盒，你解决了 "可追溯" 的问题：AI 用了哪条知识、走了哪个流程，你一清二楚。但还没解决 "有我" 的问题。

### 典型场景



* 有知识库了，AI 写的东西能溯源到你喂给它的案例

* 输出物专业、完整、没有错误

* 但客户看完说："挺好的，但不是我要的那个感觉"

### 怎么升级

把你的文风、故事、判断清单、经验包挂载上去，进入**明盒**阶段。

## English

A state where the AI's internal reasoning, knowledge sources, and SOP steps are fully visible and traceable. Output is stable, explainable, and auditable — but lacks the creator's personal voice, experiential judgment, and storytelling. Deliverables are "correct but bland": like a competent intern's work — professional but without your fingerprint.

### Why "White Box"

Borrowed from software engineering's "white-box testing" — the box is transparent; you can see the logic inside. Moving from Blind Box to White Box solves the "traceability" problem: you know exactly which knowledge the AI used and which steps it followed. But it hasn't yet solved the "me" problem.

### Typical scenarios



* You have a knowledge base; AI output traces back to cases you fed it

* Output is professional, complete, error-free

* But clients say: "It's good, but not quite the feeling I wanted"

### How to level up

Mount your writing voice, stories, judgment checklists, and experience packs — moving to **Clear Box**.
