# 场景闭环

## 中文

知识从场景中来、加工成技能、回到场景中用、再由反馈更新知识的完整循环。知识脱离场景就用不出来；闭环保证知识始终对准真实问题。

## English

The complete cycle: knowledge comes from a scenario, is processed into a skill, returns to the scenario for use, and feedback updates the knowledge. Knowledge detached from scenarios can't be applied; the loop keeps knowledge aligned with real problems.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 遇到问题→调取知识技能
- 解决→复盘更新
