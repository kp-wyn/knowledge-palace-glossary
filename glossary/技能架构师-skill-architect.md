# 技能架构师

## 中文

在AI时代，把真实业务与知识场景的问题，通过技能架构、流水线编排和系统集成，转化为可复用、可运行、可进化的技能系统的角色。

### 为什么需要这个角色

工具人人会用，但把业务沉淀成技能系统需要专门角色。技能架构师是AI时代的新职业——不是prompt工程师，不是AI产品经理，是"把一个人脑子里的经验变成AI能调用的系统"的翻译官。

### 核心能力

- **场景洞察**：识别哪些工作可以蒸馏成技能
- **技能设计**：把SOP写成AI能执行的挂载技能
- **流水线编排**：串联多个技能完成复杂任务
- **系统沉淀**：让技能可复用、可迭代、可传承

### 能力公式

> 场景洞察 × 技能设计 × 流水线编排 × 系统沉淀

## English

In the AI era, a role that transforms real business and knowledge-scenario problems into reusable, runnable, evolvable skill systems through skill architecture, pipeline orchestration, and system integration.

### Why this role exists

Everyone can use tools, but distilling business into a skill system requires a dedicated role. The Skill Architect is a new profession of the AI era — not a prompt engineer, not an AI product manager, but a translator who turns "what's in one person's head" into "a system AI can call."

### Core capabilities

- **Scenario insight**: identify which work can be distilled into skills
- **Skill design**: write SOPs as mountable skills AI can execute
- **Pipeline orchestration**: chain multiple skills to complete complex tasks
- **System precipitation**: make skills reusable, iterable, and transferable
