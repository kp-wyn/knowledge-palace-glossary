# 验规

## 中文

验证内容是否符合法律法规、行业规范与平台规则。规未合不放行——违规内容（极限词、虚假宣传等）一旦发布即带来处罚风险。

## English

Verifying that content complies with laws, industry regulations, and platform rules. No rule compliance, no release — violating content (superlatives, false advertising) triggers penalties upon publication.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 广告法极限词检查 / Advertising law extreme-term check
- 行业规范、平台规则核对 / Industry standards, platform rules verification
