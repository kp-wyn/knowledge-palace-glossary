# AI时代知识价值

## 中文

AI时代知识的价值从"占有信息"转向"调用、加工与体系化"，真正值钱的是判断、体系与经验。信息和标准答案变得廉价，价值迁移到AI难以替代的部分。

## English

In the AI era, knowledge value shifts from "possessing information" to "invoking, processing, and systematizing." What's truly valuable is judgment, systems, and experience. Information and standard answers become cheap; value migrates to what AI can't replace.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 从"我知道多少"转向"我能调用什么、解决什么"
