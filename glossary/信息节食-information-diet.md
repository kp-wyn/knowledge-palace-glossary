# 信息节食

## 中文

主动控制信息摄入的数量与质量，又称信息源治理。信息过载时，输入质量决定输出质量；不节食，采集箱被低质信息淹没、注意力被耗尽。

## English

Actively controlling the quantity and quality of information intake, also called information source governance. When overwhelmed, input quality determines output quality — without a diet, the inbox drowns in low-quality content.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 高管控制信息源、取关低质渠道 / Executives control sources, unsubscribe low-quality channels
- 固定高质量输入 / Fixed high-quality input
