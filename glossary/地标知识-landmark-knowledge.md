# 地标知识

## 中文

经过验真的高可信度知识，是宫殿中的"地标建筑"，可被放心引用。海量信息里必须有一批可信基准，否则输出无可靠参照。

## English

High-trust knowledge that has passed truth verification — the "landmark buildings" of the palace, safe to cite. Among vast information, there must be a set of trusted baselines; otherwise output has no reliable reference.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 经典理论、行业标准 / Classic theories, industry standards
- 权威机构发布、多次验证的方法论 / Authoritative methods, repeatedly verified
