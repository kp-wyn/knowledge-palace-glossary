# KP-4+1

## 中文

知识宫殿的五层架构缩写：4层楼层 + 1个贯穿层。四层是地基层、结构层、加工层、调控层；+1层是人机协同层——不是一个楼层，而是贯穿所有楼层的协同机制：AI施工队在每一层干活，每一层的验收权都在人手里。

## English

The five-layer architecture of the Knowledge Palace: 4 floors + 1 cross-cutting layer. The four floors are Foundation, Structure, Processing, and Regulation. The +1 layer is Human-AI Collaboration — not a floor, but a mechanism running through all floors: AI works on every floor, but approval stays with humans.

## 版本形态 / Editions

KP-4+1 是个人与组织共用的知识操作系统，按使用主体分两版：

- **个人版**：个人知识操作系统，对外称“个人 AI 工作平台”，服务个人学习、工作与生活；
- **组织版**：组织知识操作系统，即“企业级 AI 操作系统”，服务多岗位、多部门与企业知识库。

KP-4+1 is the knowledge operating system shared by individuals and organizations, in two editions by subject:

- **Personal edition**: personal knowledge OS, externally “personal AI workbench,” serving personal learning, work and life;
- **Organizational edition**: organizational knowledge OS — the “enterprise AI operating system” — serving multiple roles, departments and the enterprise knowledge base.
