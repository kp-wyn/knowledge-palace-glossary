# 看板

## 中文

汇集库存结构、卡片动态、技能模块、待办日程与健康度的可视化总控台。需要一个总控台汇集分散信息，支撑日常调度与决策。

## English

A visual control console aggregating inventory structure, card activity, skill modules, pending tasks, and health metrics. A central console gathers scattered information to support daily scheduling and decisions.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 一屏看库存、卡片、技能
- 看待办、健康度并下钻
