# 责任锚定

## 中文

明确每件产出的责任人，AI不承担责任，责任始终锚定到具体的人。AI可以生成内容却不能承担后果；不锚定责任人，出错无人负责。

## English

Explicitly assigning a responsible person to every output. AI doesn't bear responsibility; accountability always anchors to a specific human. AI can generate content but can't face consequences — without anchoring, no one owns errors.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 每件对外产出指定负责人、署名
