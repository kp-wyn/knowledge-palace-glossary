# 上下文

## 中文

人机协同中，人类向 AI 施工队交代的背景信息：目标、场景、边界、可用素材。AI 没有上下文就只能猜；上下文给得越准，AI 输出越贴需求，人类验收越省力。上下文是人机协同质量的前提，也是责任锚定的输入。

## English

In human-AI collaboration, the background a human hands to the AI construction team: goal, scenario, boundary, and available materials. Without context the AI can only guess; the sharper the context, the closer the output, and the lighter the human verification. Context is a prerequisite for collaboration quality and an input for responsibility anchoring.
