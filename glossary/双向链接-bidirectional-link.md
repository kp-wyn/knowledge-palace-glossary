# 双向链接

## 中文

两张卡片可互相点击跳转的链接，是卡片盒方法在宫殿中的转译。单向收藏无法让知识互相激活；双链让卡片形成网络，用时可聚拢相关卡。

## English

Links between cards that allow mutual click-through — the palace's translation of the Zettelkasten method. One-way bookmarks can't cross-activate knowledge; bidirectional links form a network that clusters related cards when needed.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 知识卡之间互设链接 / Cards link to each other bidirectionally
- 知识卡与经验卡之间互链 / Knowledge cards and experience cards link to each other
