# 流水线技能

## 中文

把多个技能按固定流程串联编排、形成端到端作业流水线的技能组合。真实业务是一连串动作，单技能无法端到端完成；流水线把方法固化成标准作业流程。

## English

A skill combination that chains multiple skills in a fixed sequence to form an end-to-end workflow. Real business is a series of actions; single skills can't handle end-to-end — pipelines harden methodology into standard operating procedures.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 行前风险预警流水线
- 建殿流水线、组卷流水线
