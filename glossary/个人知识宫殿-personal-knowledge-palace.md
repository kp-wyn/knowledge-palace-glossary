# 个人知识宫殿

## 中文

服务个人学习、工作与生活的知识宫殿，强调个人主导、个人私密与个人成长。职场人需要一套属于自己、可迁移、可成长的知识系统。

## English

A Knowledge Palace serving personal learning, work, and life — emphasizing personal ownership, privacy, and growth. Professionals need a knowledge system that belongs to them, is portable, and grows with them.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 个人学习、职业输出
- 生活事务的统一管理
