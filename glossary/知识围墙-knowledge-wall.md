# 知识围墙

## 中文

每个知识宫殿的边界定义，明确哪些知识属于本宫殿，哪些属于外部。没有围墙，知识无限膨胀、权属不清，宫殿失去整体性。

## English

The boundary definition of each Knowledge Palace — what knowledge belongs inside and what stays outside. Without walls, knowledge bloats indefinitely, ownership is unclear, and the palace loses integrity.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 划定个人、企业、裙房各自边界 / Define boundaries for personal, enterprise, and annex
- 决定什么能进、什么不进 / Decide what enters and what doesn't
