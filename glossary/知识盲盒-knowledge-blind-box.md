# 知识盲盒

## 中文

挂载了技能、Agent、工作流、SOP、专家模板后AI生成内容的状态——方向可控、结构稳定，但70分基线水平浮动，偶尔能蹦出90分作品。核心症状是**产出物不属于你**：不是你的文风、不是你的经验沉淀、不是你的判断痕迹，你只是旁观者和验收员。

### 为什么需要这个词

很多人以为"会写prompt"就是会用AI了。其实你只是从黑盒走进了盲盒。你知道会开出什么系列（结构对了、方向对了），但款式是随机的（质量不稳定、没有你的指纹）。而且开出来的手办不是你做的。

### 典型场景

- 用了各种AI写作模板，出来的东西"还行但不是我"
- AI写得很快，但你要花大量时间改文风、加自己的案例
- 看完输出物，你怀疑这真的是"我"写的吗？

### 怎么升级

把自己的方法论、案例、知识库喂给AI，让它调用的是你验证过的东西，进入**白盒**阶段。

## English

The state of AI output after mounting skills, agents, workflows, SOPs, or expert templates. Direction is controllable and structure is stable, but quality hovers around a 70/100 baseline with occasional 90-point surprises. The core symptom: **the output isn't yours** — it lacks your writing voice, your accumulated experience, your judgment. You're merely an observer and approver.

### Why this term exists

Many people think "knowing how to write prompts" means they've mastered AI. In reality, they've just moved from Black Box to Blind Box. You know which series you're getting (correct structure, right direction), but the variant is random (unstable quality, no fingerprint). And the collectible figure wasn't made by you.

### Typical scenarios

- Using AI writing templates: output is "okay but not me"
- AI writes fast, but you spend hours adjusting tone and adding your own cases
- After reading the output, you doubt whether it could really be "you"

### How to level up

Feed your own methodology, cases, and knowledge base into the AI so it calls upon what you've validated — moving to **White Box**.
