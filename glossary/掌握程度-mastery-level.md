# 掌握程度

## 中文

知识卡的附加字段，标注对该知识的掌握等级：了解、理解、应用、精通（四级）。同样"存过"的知识，掌握深浅差别极大；标注等级才能区分哪些真会用。

## English

An additional field on knowledge cards marking mastery: aware, understand, apply, master (four levels). Stored knowledge varies wildly in depth; levels distinguish what you can actually use from what you merely saw.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 给每张知识卡标掌握等级 / Tag each card with a mastery level
- 按等级安排复习 / Schedule review by level
