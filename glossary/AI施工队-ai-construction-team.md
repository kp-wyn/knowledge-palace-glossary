# AI施工队

## 中文

承担执行的AI角色集合，含采集员、拆解员、制图员、质检员、书记员等，在各层完成具体作业。大量重复性、机械性执行工作交给AI，把人的精力留给判断与创造。

## English

A collection of AI roles that execute work — collector, disassembler, chart-maker, quality checker, scribe — performing concrete tasks on every floor. Repetitive mechanical work goes to AI; human energy is reserved for judgment and creation.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 采集、拆解、制图、压缩 / Collect, decompose, diagram, compress
- 初稿、格式转换、数据计算 / Drafts, format conversion, data computation
