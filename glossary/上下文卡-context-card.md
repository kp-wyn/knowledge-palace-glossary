# 上下文卡

## 中文

人机协同中专门记录任务背景的卡片类型：目标、场景、边界、可用素材，随任务一起交给 AI 施工队。AI 没有上下文就只能猜；把背景写成卡，交代得越准，AI 输出越贴需求，人类验收越省力。上下文卡是责任锚定的输入，也是场景闭环的起点。

## English

A card type dedicated to recording task background in human-AI collaboration: goal, scenario, boundary, and available materials, handed to the AI construction team along with the task. Without context the AI can only guess; writing the background into a card makes briefing sharp, output closer to the need, and human verification lighter. The Context Card is an input for responsibility anchoring and the starting point of the scenario loop.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 给 AI 施工队下任务前先写一张上下文卡 / Write a Context Card before tasking the AI team
- 场景闭环的输入 / Input of the scenario loop
- 责任锚定的前提 / Prerequisite of responsibility anchoring
