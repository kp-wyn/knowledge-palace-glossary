# 验合规

## 中文

对内容做整体合规性审查，覆盖法律、广告、行业与平台规则的合规判断。验规聚焦具体规则，验合规做整体把关，确保对外输出整体合法、不越界。

## English

Overall compliance review covering legal, advertising, industry, and platform rules. Rule check focuses on specific regulations; compliance check ensures the entire output is lawful and within bounds.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 发布前整体合规审查 / Overall compliance review before publishing
- 持证岗位专业合规审查 / Licensed-role professional compliance review
