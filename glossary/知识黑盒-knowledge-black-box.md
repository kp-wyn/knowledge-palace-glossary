# 知识黑盒

## 中文

裸AI在没有任何技能、SOP、方法论挂载的状态下生成内容——内部推理过程不可见，输出结果不可预测，概率论上第一次产出通常不可用。

### 为什么需要这个词

大部分人刚用AI时都在这一层。你问"帮我写个方案"，它吐出来一坨，你改，它再吐，你再改。本质上你在抽卡。这个词把这种"失控感"命名出来——不是你不会用AI，是你站在了黑盒外面。

### 典型场景

- 直接问ChatGPT/豆包："帮我写个品牌方案"
- 第一次出来的东西没法用，反复抽五六次才碰到一个能用的
- 同样的提示词，每次结果不一样，无法复用

### 怎么升级

加角色设定、背景、约束、输出格式（四要素），进入**盲盒**阶段。

## English

An AI-generated output produced without any mounted skills, SOPs, or methodology frameworks. The internal reasoning process is opaque and results are unpredictable — in practice, the first output is rarely usable.

### Why this term exists

Most people using AI for the first time are at this stage. You ask for a proposal, get something unusable, iterate, and repeat. You're essentially pulling slots. This term names that feeling of loss of control — it's not that you don't know how to use AI; it's that you're standing outside the box.

### Typical scenarios

- Asking ChatGPT/Doubao directly: "write me a brand proposal"
- The first output is unusable; you iterate 5-6 times before finding one that works
- The same prompt yields different results each time; nothing is reusable

### How to level up

Add role setting, context, constraints, and output format (the four elements) to move to **Blind Box**.
