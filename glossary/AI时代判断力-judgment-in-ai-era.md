# AI时代判断力

## 中文

在AI能快速给出答案的时代，人最稀缺的能力：判断信息真伪、方案优劣与价值取舍。AI负责生成，人负责判断；判断力无法外包，是人机分工中人的核心价值。

## English

In an era where AI gives answers quickly, the scarcest human capability: judging truth, solution quality, and value trade-offs. AI generates; humans judge. Judgment cannot be outsourced — it is humanity's core value in human-AI collaboration.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 判断AI产出对不对
- 判断方案选哪个、什么值得做
