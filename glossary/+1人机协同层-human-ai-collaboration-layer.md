# 人机协同层

## 中文

贯穿所有楼层的协同机制——AI施工队做执行，人类做决策与验收。它不是第五层：AI在每一层参与执行却不单独成层，人始终是责任主体。

## English

A cross-cutting mechanism running through all floors — the AI construction team executes; humans decide and approve. It's not a fifth floor: AI participates on every floor but doesn't form its own layer. Humans remain the responsible party.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- AI采集、拆解、制图、出初稿
- 人定方向、做判断、质量终审
