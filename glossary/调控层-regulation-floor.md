# 调控层

## 中文

知识宫殿的第四层，负责日巡检、周复盘、月审计、更新淘汰与架构治理。知识有半衰期，不调控的宫殿半年就变成知识垃圾场。

## English

The fourth floor, responsible for daily inspection, weekly review, monthly audit, pruning, and architecture governance. Knowledge has a half-life; without regulation, the palace becomes a knowledge junkyard in six months.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 日清缓冲区、周复盘技能效果
- 月审计打标签
- 季度重构房间、盘点价值
