# Agent失控

## 中文

当组织里 Agent（以及技能）越来越多，却缺乏身份、权限、审计与异常治理时出现的**失控状态**：身份混乱、权限失控、审计缺失、异常无人管、数据泄露。它是技能治理层要解决的**问题 / 反模式**，也是知识宫殿“可控可管”价值的对照面。

属行业现象（报道命题），由知识宫殿体系引用并给出治理框架，**非体系原创**。

## English

The **runaway state** when an organization’s agents (and skills) multiply without governance of identity, permissions, audit and exceptions: confused identity, uncontrolled permissions, missing audit, unhandled exceptions, data leakage. It is the **problem / anti-pattern** the Skill Governance Layer addresses, and the counterpoint to the Knowledge Palace’s “controllable and manageable” value.

An industry phenomenon (the article’s premise), cited by the Knowledge Palace with a governance framework — **not original to the system**.
