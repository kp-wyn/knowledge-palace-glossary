# 知识宫殿

## 中文

一套个人与组织知识操作系统——把你脑子里的经验、方法论、判断，变成结构化、可挂载、可被AI调用的系统。

### 它解决什么问题

AI时代最大的浪费不是"我不会用AI"，而是"我的经验只存在我脑子里，AI用不上"。知识宫殿就是把你的经验从脑子里搬到一个结构化的系统里，让AI能调用、能复用、能交付。

### 与"记忆宫殿"的区别

| 维度 | 记忆宫殿（Memory Palace） | 知识宫殿（Knowledge Palace） |
|---|---|---|
| 起源 | 古希腊记忆术（Loci法） | AI时代知识管理方法论 |
| 目的 | 记住更多信息 | 让AI调用你的经验交付 |
| 载体 | 大脑/想象中的空间 | 数字系统+AI技能挂载 |
| 产出 | 记忆能力 | 可交付的工作成果 |
| 核心 | 空间联想记忆 | 四层架构+三验一密+技能蒸馏 |

### 核心结构

- **地基层**：采集、存储原始素材
- **结构层**：分类、建模、关联
- **加工层**：提取知识、蒸馏技能、组合创新
- **调控层**：复盘、迭代、淘汰
- **+1人机协同层**：AI做执行，人做决策和验收

### 适合谁

- 做了10年以上、脑子里有货但没沉淀的专业人士
- 想把个人经验变成可复用产品的专家
- 想建企业知识库的团队

## English

A personal and organizational knowledge operating system — turning the experience, methodology, and judgment in your head into a structured, mountable, AI-callable system.

### The problem it solves

The biggest waste in the AI era isn't "I don't know how to use AI" — it's "my experience only exists in my head, and AI can't access it." The Knowledge Palace moves your experience from your brain into a structured system that AI can call, reuse, and deliver.

### Difference from "Memory Palace"

| Dimension | Memory Palace | Knowledge Palace |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Ancient Greek mnemonic (Method of Loci) | AI-era knowledge management methodology |
| Purpose | Remember more information | Let AI call your experience to deliver work |
| Medium | Brain / imagined space | Digital system + AI skill mounting |
| Output | Memory ability | Deliverable work products |
| Core | Spatial association | 4-layer architecture + 3-verification + skill distillation |

### Core structure

- **Foundation**: collect and store raw materials
- **Structure**: classify, model, link
- **Processing**: extract knowledge, distill skills, innovate
- **Regulation**: review, iterate, prune
- **+1 Human-AI Layer**: AI executes; humans decide and approve
