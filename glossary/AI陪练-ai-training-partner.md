# AI陪练

## 中文

承担反方质疑、模拟问答、辅助分析、提供选项的AI角色，用于刻意练习与压力测试。能力需要在被质疑、被追问中检验和强化。

## English

An AI role that plays devil's advocate, simulates Q&A, assists analysis, and provides options — for deliberate practice and stress testing. Capability is tested and strengthened through challenge and follow-up questioning.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 模拟客户提问
- 当反方质疑方案、面试演练
