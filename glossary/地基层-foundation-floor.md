# 地基层

## 中文

知识宫殿的第一层，负责原始素材的采集、编码与存储。地基决定宫殿能建多高——素材只被随手丢进收藏夹，后面楼层就无米下锅。

## English

The first floor of the Knowledge Palace, responsible for collecting, encoding, and storing raw materials. The foundation determines how high the palace can rise — if materials are just tossed into bookmarks, the upper floors have nothing to work with.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 读书划线、网页剪藏进入采集箱 / Book highlights, web clips to intake box
- 聊天记录、会议纪要、临时灵感归位 / Chats, meeting notes, fleeting ideas filed
