# 验密

## 中文

三验一密的第四道（即"一密"），验证敏感信息的脱敏程度。密未脱不放行——对外输出一旦带隐私、机密、个人信息，会造成泄露与法律风险。

## English

The fourth gate (the "one encryption"), verifying the desensitization of sensitive information. No desensitization, no release — outbound content with privacy, confidential, or personal data causes leaks and legal risk.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 隐去姓名、证件、联系方式 / Redact names, IDs, contact info
- 隐去客户信息与商业机密 / Redact client info and trade secrets
