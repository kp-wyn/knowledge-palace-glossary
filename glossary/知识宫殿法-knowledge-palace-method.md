# 知识宫殿法

## 中文

搭建和运营知识宫殿的完整方法论 —— 从知识采集、结构建模、技能蒸馏到复盘迭代的全流程操作指南。它不是一个软件，是一套 "怎么把脑子里的东西变成 AI 能用的资产" 的操作系统。

### 核心原则



1. **先有真知识，再谈 AI**—— 没有验证为真的知识卡，AI 就是在幻觉

2. **经验必须蒸馏成技能**—— 散的经验用不上，蒸馏成流水线才能复用

3. **人做决策，AI 做执行**——+1 层的本质是人机分工

4. **三验一密是底线**—— 验真、验疑、验错 + 加密溯源

### 五步搭建法



1. **采集**：把你读过、用过、验真过的知识存进来

2. **建模**：分类、关联、形成你的知识体系

3. **蒸馏**：把经验变成知识卡和可挂载技能

4. **挂载**：让 AI 调用你的知识和技能

5. **迭代**：复盘、淘汰、升级

## English

The complete methodology for building and operating a Knowledge Palace — from knowledge collection, structural modeling, and skill distillation to review and iteration. It's not a software; it's an operating system for "turning what's in your head into assets AI can use."

### Core principles



1. **Real knowledge before AI** — without validated knowledge cards, AI is hallucinating

2. **Experience must be distilled into skills** — scattered experience can't be reused; distilled pipelines can

3. **Humans decide, AI executes** — the +1 layer is fundamentally about human-AI division of labor

4. **3-verification + encryption is the baseline** — verify truth, doubt, and error + encrypted traceability

### Five-step building method



1. **Collect**: store what you've read, used, and validated

2. **Model**: classify, link, form your knowledge system

3. **Distill**: turn experience into knowledge cards and mountable skills

4. **Mount**: let AI call your knowledge and skills

5. **Iterate**: review, prune, upgrade
