# 验真

## 中文

三验一密的第一道，验证知识的真实性、来源可靠性与时效性。真未验不放行——假知识一旦入库会污染后续所有输出。

## English

The first gate of Three Verifications and One Encryption, verifying the truthfulness, source reliability, and timeliness of knowledge. No truth verification, no entry — false knowledge pollutes everything downstream.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 核对一条知识的出处、时间、是否仍有效
- 对权威来源做评级
