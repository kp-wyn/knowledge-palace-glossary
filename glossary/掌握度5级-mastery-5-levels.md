# 掌握度5级

## 中文

知识资产账本中对掌握程度的五级自评标尺：L0未学、L1了解、L2理解、L3能用、L4能教。账本需要更细、含"未学、能教"两端的标尺来盘点资产。

## English

The five-level self-assessment scale in the Knowledge Asset Ledger: L0 not learned, L1 aware, L2 understand, L3 can use, L4 can teach. The ledger needs a finer scale covering both ends — from "not learned" to "can teach."

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 账本里给每项资产评L0-L4
- 按等级安排转化
