# AI时代体系感

## 中文

在碎片化信息与点状答案中，人对整体结构、要素关系与因果链路的把握能力。AI给的是点状答案，人若没有体系就无法把答案组织成系统。

## English

Amid fragmented information and point-like answers, the human ability to grasp overall structure, element relationships, and causal chains. AI gives point answers; without systems thinking, humans can't organize them into a whole.

## 典型场景 / Typical Scenarios

- 看清一个问题的整体结构
- 看清要素如何关联
